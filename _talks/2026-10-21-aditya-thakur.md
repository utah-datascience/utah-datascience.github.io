There are many applications where deep neural networks (DNNs) need to satisfy constraints, e.g., safety-critical domains or scientific machine learning. Testing and verification can determine whether DNN satisfies a constraint. This talk addresses the next step of this process: what to do once faulty behavior is identified. I will discuss recent advances in the problem of provable editing of DNNs. Given a trained DNN and a specification, provable editing makes small changes to the parameters of the DNN to guarantee that the edited DNN satisfies the given specification while still ensuring high accuracy. I will discuss efficient algorithms for this problem that support a wide range of DNN architectures and edit specifications. Our techniques have been applied to ACAS Xu, ImageNet ResNet DNNs, Vision Transformers, Llama, and Qwen models.
