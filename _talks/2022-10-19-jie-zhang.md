Models satisfying Min-max fairness minimizes maximum group specific losses, so that the model has a more equitable performance over all groups. Benefit of min-max fair models over other fairness notions and models include it levels up: meaning it only degrades performance of a group if the degradation improves performance on the worst off group.

In this talk, I will briefly mention the prior works that defined min-max fairness. I will mainly focus on our paper “Active Sampling for Min-Max Fairness” to introduce two algorithms that find min-max fair models with convergence guarantees.
